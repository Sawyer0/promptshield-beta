The Principle of Data Minimization 

Only collect and store what we absolutely need, and only keep it as long as necessary. 

This means making storage configurable rather than mandatory. 

Implement multiple storage modes: 

●  A full audit mode that retains everything 
●  A metrics-only mode that keeps aggregate statistics but not the actual prompts 
●  And an ephemeral mode that performs scans without any persistent storage 

Here's how we might structure this choice in our configuration: 

data_retention: 

mode: "metrics-only" ( Options: "full-audit", "metrics-only", "ephemeral") 

retention_days: 90 ( How long to keep data (only applies to non-ephemeral modes) ) 

auto_purge: true ( Automatically delete old data) 

This approach lets security-conscious organizations choose their comfort level while still 
providing the functionality they need.
