Audit Trails and Monitoring 

Every scan operation should generate an audit record that includes not just what was scanned 
and what was found, but also: 

●  Contextual information like the user who performed the scan 
●  The source of the prompt (if available) 
●  And the specific RulePack and models used 

The audit logs themselves also need protection. If an attacker can modify or delete audit 
records, they can cover their tracks and compromise our entire security posture. 

Implement append-only audit logs with cryptographic integrity checks, or use tamper-evident 
logging systems that make unauthorized modifications detectable. 

What should trigger security alerts in your system. Obviously, detected security violations in 
prompts should be logged, but maybe also: 

●  Alerting on unusual patterns like a sudden spike in scanning activity 
●  Repeated failures to access external APIs 
●  Attempts to scan from unusual locations or times
