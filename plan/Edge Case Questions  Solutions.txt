Edge Case Questions & Solutions 

●  What happens if a developer accidentally scans a prompt containing customer credit 

card numbers? 

How does our system detect this, what warnings does it provide, and how do we ensure this 
sensitive data is handled appropriately? 

●  Since we plan for our tool to be deployed in CI/CD pipeline's that process hundreds of 
prompts per day, how do we balance the need for comprehensive audit trails with 
storage costs and performance requirements? 

●  How would we handle a situation where an organization wants to use our tool but has 

policies preventing any data from leaving their network, even encrypted data sent to LLM 
APIs?
