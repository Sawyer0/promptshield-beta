Privacy by Design Considerations 

Privacy protection goes beyond just security controls - it requires thinking carefully about what 
data you collect and how you use it. 

This becomes especially important as privacy regulations like GDPR and CCPA create legal 
obligations around data handling. 

● 

Implement data classification within the tool 

When scanning prompts, our RulePacks will not only detect security issues but also identify and 
classify sensitive data types like : 

●  PII 
●  Financial information 
●  Health records 
●  etc. 

This classification will then drive automatic retention policies, access controls, and notification 
requirements. 

For metrics and analytics purposes, we don't need the actual prompt content. Only the 
information about what types of patterns were detected and when. 

● 

Implement automatic anonymization for time-series data 

Replacing actual prompt content with generic placeholders while preserving the 
security-relevant characteristics. 

Under various privacy regulations, users may have the right to request deletion of their data. 

Our tool should provide mechanisms for identifying and purging all data associated with specific 

●  Prompts 
●  Users 
●  Time periods
