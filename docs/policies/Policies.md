### Core Policies (Starter Set)

1) Information Security Policy
- Objectives, scope, roles, risk mgmt, exceptions, review cadence (annual)

2) Access Control Policy
- SSO/MFA required; JML workflow; quarterly access reviews; least privilege; break‑glass

3) Change Management Policy
- PR reviews required; tracked change tickets; CAB weekly; emergency changes post‑review

4) Secure SDLC Policy
- Threat modeling for major changes; SAST/DAST; dep scanning; code review checklist; secrets scanning

5) Incident Response Policy
- Severity matrix; on‑call; comms; 5‑Whys postmortems within 5 business days; exercises quarterly

6) Vulnerability Management Policy
- Weekly scans; SLA: Critical 7d, High 30d; patch windows; pen test annually

7) Business Continuity & DR Policy
- RTO/RPO; backup schedule; quarterly restore tests; annual DR exercise; alternate region strategy

8) Vendor Management Policy
- Security review, DPA, right‑to‑audit; annual reassessment

9) Data Classification & Retention Policy
- Classes (Public/Internal/Restricted); retention schedules; purge procedures

10) Logging & Monitoring Policy
- Centralized logs, 1‑year retention; alerting rules; runbooks and evidence retention
